What is AI?

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence.

Imagine if your toys could play with you all by themselves! AI is like giving a computer a super smart brain so it can do fun things, like talking to you, playing games, or even drawing pictures.

🧠 ✨ 🎮

What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is how we teach the AI.

Just like you learn to ride a bike by practicing, a computer learns by looking at lots of examples! If we show it 100 pictures of cats, it learns what a cat looks like!
